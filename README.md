## Key Findings

1. Missing values were mainly found in new_price, power, and engine columns. Missingness was related to vehicle listing information and availability.

2. The IQR method detected more outliers than the Z-score method because vehicle prices were right-skewed.

3. Vehicle price was most strongly affected by power, engine size, and vehicle specifications.
